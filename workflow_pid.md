# Nigeria Criminal Justice Process — How E Dey Waka Step-by-Step

## Stage: Arrest (Police Carry Person)

**Wetin dey happen:**
Police or other law enforcement officers fit carry person if court order dey, or if dem get strong reason to suspect say the person do crime. Dem suppose tell you say dem don arrest you and explain why dem carry you—unless say matter for ground serious well-well. Dem no suppose use over-force or beat you unless e really necessary to hold you. This na where everything start, and your rights start to work from this moment.

**Wetin you go expect:**
- **Who go dey there:** One or more police, sometimes dem fit wear normal cloth (plainclothes) depending on their unit.
- **Wetin dem go ask you:** Simple identity questions like your name and where you dey stay. Deep questioning suppose happen for station, no be for road.
- **Wetin dem fit decide:** Whether to carry you go station direct, if dem go use handcuff, or if dem go tell you the crime for road or for station.

**Your rights for this stage:**
- You get right say dem no fit just arrest you anyhow without law (Section 35(1)).
- Right to know why dem arrest you, for language wey you understand (Section 35(3)).
- **Right to keep quiet** and no talk anything (Section 35(2)).
- Right to better treatment—make dem no use wicked force (Section 34(1)).

**Wetin you fit talk:**
- "Wetin be the reason wey you take arrest me?"
- "I no too sabi English—abeg explain for [Yoruba/Hausa/Pidgin]."
- "I no go talk anything until I see my lawyer."


## Stage: For Police Station (Investigation & Statement)

**Wetin dey happen:**
When you reach station, dem fit search you, ask you questions, and tell you make you write statement about wetin happen. This na the place where many people rights dey get issues—like when dem force person talk wetin e no do, or keep person pass the time wey law gree. If the case no big pass, police fit give you "police bail" for here without going to court.

**Wetin you go expect:**
- **Who go dey there:** The Investigating Police Officer (IPO), your lawyer (if dem dey), and person to translate if you no understand.
- **Wetin dem go ask you:** Details about the crime, your background, and where you dey when the thing happen.
- **Wetin dem fit decide:** If dem go give you police bail, if dem go carry matter go court, or if dem go lock you down.

**Your rights for this stage:**
- Right to get person wey go translate for you (Section 36(6)(e)).
- Right to carry your lawyer come, even when you dey write statement (Section 36(6)(c)).
- Right to keep quiet—if dem force you write statement, the court fit reject am later (Evidence Act Section 29(2)).
- Right to be carried go court within the correct time if dem no release you (Section 35(4)-(5)).

**Wetin you fit talk:**
- "I want make my lawyer dey here before I answer any question."
- "I no go sign this statement until you read am give me for language wey I understand."
- "I fit get police bail for this matter?"


## Stage: First Court Appearance (Arraignment)

**Wetin dey happen:**
If the matter serious, dem go carry the person (wey dem don dey call "defendant" now) go court. The court go read the charge loud-loud and ask you if you "guilty" or "not guilty." You must dey there physical—lawyer no fit stand for you if you no dey.

**Wetin you go expect:**
- **Who go dey there:** Judge or Magistrate, Prosecutor, Court Clerk, your lawyer, and normal people (public) because court na open place.
- **Wetin dem go ask you:** If you understand the charge and how you plead (Guilty or Not Guilty).
- **Wetin dem fit decide:** If dem go give you bail, if dem go shift the case go another day (adjournment), or if dem go accept your plea.

**Your rights for this stage:**
- Right to know every detail about the charge for language wey you sabi (Section 36(6)(a)).
- Right to get lawyer and make the hearing be for open court (Section 36(3)-(4)).
- Right to ask for bail.

**Wetin you fit talk:**
- "I no really understand this charge—abeg explain am again."
- "I plead 'not guilty', and I want apply for bail."
- "I need small time make I get lawyer before I talk anything."


## Stage: Bail (Pending Trial)

**Wetin dey happen:**
Whether for station or court, you fit ask make dem release you small while the case dey go on. Usually, dem go ask for "surety" (person wey go sign for you) or "bond." Court go check if the crime big, if you go run, or if you go scatter the case. Bail conditions no suppose too hard say e go be like dem still lock you up.

**Wetin you go expect:**
- **Who go dey there:** Judge, Prosecutor, Lawyer, and your surety.
- **Wetin dem go ask you:** Your connection for area, if your surety get level to bring you come back, and if you get money for the conditions.
- **Wetin dem fit decide:** Bail without condition, bail with conditions (like passport or surety), or dem fit deny bail if the matter too serious.

**Your rights for this stage:**
- Law assume say you never do wrong until dem prove am, so you get right to bail (Section 36(5)).
- Right say bail conditions no suppose wicked.
- Right make dem no lock you pass the time wey law talk if bail delay.

**Wetin you fit talk:**
- "I want apply for bail—wetin be the conditions wey court go gree?"
- "This bail condition too hard for me—abeg make court review am."
- "Somebody ask me for money for bail—I want report say this one no good."


## Stage: Trial (Proper Case)

**Wetin dey happen:**
If you talk say you no be guilty, the real trial go start. Prosecution (police or govt) go bring their witnesses and evidence first; your lawyer go ask dem questions (cross-examine). After that, you fit talk say the case no get head (no case to answer) or bring your own witnesses.

**Wetin you go expect:**
- **Who go dey there:** Judge, Prosecutor, your lawyer, witnesses, and the public.
- **Wetin dem go ask you:** If you choose to talk, your lawyer and the prosecutor go ask you questions—but nobody fit force you to talk.
- **Wetin dem fit decide:** If the case go end because prosecution no get proof, wetin go be evidence, and if you guilty or not.

**Your rights for this stage:**
- Right to fair hearing from judge wey no side anybody (Section 36(1)).
- Right to trial wey go finish for reasonable time.
- Right make nobody force you to talk against yourself (Section 36(11)).
- Right to challenge any confession wey dem force you talk.

**Wetin you fit talk:**
- "I want make dem explain wetin this witness talk before we continue."
- "I no want talk anything for this case (I do not wish to testify)."
- "This trial don delay too much—I want make court note am."


## Stage: Judgment & Sentencing (Last Result)

**Wetin dey happen:**
After everybody don talk, the judge go give decision—Guilty or Not Guilty—and e must talk am for public. If you guilty, the judge fit give sentence (punishment) that day or another day.

**Wetin you go expect:**
- **Who go dey there:** Judge, Prosecutor, your lawyer, and public.
- **Wetin dem go ask you:** Your lawyer fit beg for small mercy (mitigation) before judge give the final sentence.
- **Wetin dem fit decide:** Release you (acquittal), give you prison time, fine, or community service.

**Your rights for this stage:**
- Right make judgment happen for open court, no be inside room (Section 36(3)).
- Right to appeal the judgment if you no gree.
- Protection make dem no try you two times for the same crime (Section 36(9)).

**Wetin you fit talk:**
- "I want make court note say I go appeal this decision."
- "Make my lawyer talk small for my side before the final sentence."


## Stage: Appeal (Higher Court)

**Wetin dey happen:**
This one no be new trial—na when higher court check if the first court follow law well. You fit need permission from court to appeal, or e fit be your right depending on the case.
