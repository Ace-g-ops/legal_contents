## Muhimman Bayanan Bayani

**Wannan manhaja ba lauya ba ce.**
Yana ba da bayanai na gaba ɗaya game da haƙƙinku, ba shawara ta shari'a ba ga takamaiman shari'arku.

**Lokacin da dole ne ku ga lauya na gaske:**
- Idan ana tuhumar ku da babban laifi
- Idan ana tsare ku kuma ba ku san abin da za ku yi ba
- Idan ana take haƙƙinku
- Idan ana neman ku sanya hannu kan takardu ba ku fahimta ba

**Yadda ake neman taimakon shari'a:**
- Cibiyar Sadarwa ta Cibiyoyin Taimakon Shari'a na Jami'a (NULAI) Najeriya — tana gudanar da asibitocin shari'a sama da 14 da ke jami'a suna ba da ayyukan shari'a kyauta ga al'ummomin da ba su da isasshen kulawa. https://nulai.org
- WACOL (Ƙungiyar Taimakon Mata) — Babban mai ba da tallafin shari'a a Najeriya musamman ga mata da 'yan mata; ofisoshi/ asibitocin tafi-da-gidanka a Katsina, Ebonyi, Anambra, Bayelsa, Benue, Enugu, Rivers. https://wacolnigeria.org
- Hukumar Kare Hakkin Dan Adam ta Ƙasa (NHRC) — hukumar tarayya don bayar da rahoton take haƙƙoƙin ɗan adam (gami da cin zarafin 'yan sanda/cin zarafin tsarewa); Lambar gajeriyar lambar kyauta 6472, ko 08006472428, Litinin-Juma'a 8am–4pm; info@nhrc.gov.ng; https://nigeriarights.gov.ng

**Ƙungiyar Lauyoyin Najeriya (NBA):**
- Yanar Gizo: nigerianbar.org.ng
- Ba a sami lambar wayar da aka tabbatar ba a yanzu - kai tsaye ga masu amfani zuwa shafin tuntuɓar gidan yanar gizon maimakon lambar da ba zan iya tabbatarwa tana aiki ba.

**Cibiyoyin shari'a kyauta:**
- Asibitin Taimakon Shari'a na Babban Taron NBA na Shekara-shekara - yana gudana kowace shekara, shawarwari na shari'a kyauta, tare da ma'aikatan sa kai waɗanda ke magana da Turanci, Yarbanci, Hausa, Igbo, da Pidgin
- Tsarin Lauyoyin 'Yan Sanda (PDSS) - yana sanya lauyoyi masu aiki ga mutanen da ke hannun 'yan sanda - waɗanda suka dace da shari'ar amfani da app ɗinku.

**Yanayin gaggawa:**
Idan kuna cikin haɗari nan take ko kuma ana yi muku rashin adalci, gaya wa wani da kuka amince da shi kuma ku nemi ya yi magana da lauya da wuri-wuri.
