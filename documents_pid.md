## Document: Charge Sheet

**Wetin e mean:**
A Charge sheet na the formal document wey list all the crimes(s) wey them accuse pesin of. E dey filed by the prosecution and them dey read am for court during pesin first appearance. The pesin get right to receive a copy within 7 days of filing/

**Legal Basis:**
Administration of Criminal Justice Act (AJCA) 2015, Sections 193-196

**When pesin go see am:**
- For hin first appearance
- When charges dey filed against am
- Anytime wey charges dey ammended or upgraded

**Key sections with wetin them mean:**

| Section | Wetin e mean | Wetin to check |
|---------|---------------|---------------|
| **Heading** | Name of the court (e.g., "IN THE MAGISTRATE COURT OF LAGOS") | Shey na the correct court for your case be this ?  |
| **Charge Number** | Reference number for the case (e.g., "CHARGE NO: ABC/123/2024") | Write this down — you'll go need am for all future court visits |
| **Parties** | Who is prosecuting you (e.g., "The People of Lagos State") and your name | Shey your name dey spelt correctly? |
| **Statement of Offence** | Name of the crime (e.g., "Stealing", "Assault", "Criminal Damage") |Shey this match wetin the police tell you? |
| **Particulars of Offence** | Details: when, where, what happened, who was affected | Shey the date, place, and details dey accurate? |
| **Section of Law** | Which law you allegedly broke (e.g., "Section 516 Criminal Code") | Ask your lawyer to explain wetin this law mean |
| **Date** | When the charge was filed | Should be soon after your arrest (usually within days) |
| **Signature** | Signature of prosecutor or police officer who filed the charge | It must dey signed — unsigned charges fit dey invalid |

**How to check if e dey correct:**
1. **Tell your lawyer kiakia** -They fit object to defective charges
2. **Ask the court say make them note any error** - The judge should be aware
3. **No sign anything** - until them apply changes to the defected documents, no sign anything
4. **Ask them for a coreected copy** - You dey entitled to accurate charge documents
5. **If charge dey completely different** - if the charges dey completlely different from wetin them arrest you for, ask your lawyer make him challenge am

**Red flags (suspicious changes wey you fit look at for):**

**High priority (could indicate tampering):** 
- Date of alleged offence wey them change without explanation
- Charge wey them upgrade to a more serious offence suddenly 
- Your name or identity details wey them don change without your consent
- - Court name changed (fit mean say case was moved without your knowledge)
- Missing signature or court stamp
- No charge number or reference

**Medium Priority:**
- Spelling mistakes wey occur for your name or address
- Wrong date format
- Minor formatting changes

**If you spot any suspicious changes:**
- Compare am with an earlier copy wey been get
- Ask your lawyer say make he verify with the ciort own registry
- Ask for certified true copies from the court
- No plead until the charge/s dey clarified

**Your rights regarding charge sheet:**
- Right to receive a copy of wetin them charge you for (AJCA 2015)
- Right to get wetin the charge mean(for language wey you yourself understand)
- Right to challenge defective or unclear charges
- Right to have the charge read to you for court

**Sample Sheet**: 


IN THE MAGISTRATE COURT OF LAGOS
CHARGE NO: ABC/123/2024

THE PEOPLE OF LAGOS STATE vs. JOHN

STATEMENT OF OFFENCE:
Stealing

PARTICULARS OF OFFENCE:
That you, John Doe, on the 15th day of January 2024, at Lagos Island, stole a mobile phone valued at ₦50,000 belonging to Mary Smith, thereby committing an offence punishable under Section 516 of the Criminal Code.

Dated this 20th day of January 2024

[Signature]

Public Prosecutor

**Sources:**
- Administration of Criminal Justice Act (ACJA) 2015, Sections 193-196
- 1999 Constitution of Nigeria, Section 36 (Right to Fair Hearing)
- FCT High Court Practice Direction on ACJA 2015


## Document: Bail Order

**Wetin e mean:**

A bail order na the court document wey set the condition whether if you fit dey release form custody while you dey wait for trial. It specifies how much money or property you yourself(or your suretiess) fit pledge, and which rules wey you fit follow while you dey on bail.


**Legal basis:**
Administration of Criminal Justice Act (ACJA) 2015, Part 19 (Sections 158-188)

**When pesin go see am:**
- After pesin first appearance when the judge fit later consider bail
- When bail don dey granted by the court
- When bail conditions dey different or dey updated
- When sureties don dey approved

**Key sections and wetin them mean:**
| Section | What it means | What to check |
|---------|---------------|---------------|
| **Heading** | Name of the court (e.g., "IN THE HIGH COURT OF LAGOS STATE") | Is this the correct court handling your case? |
| **Case Number** | Reference number for your case (e.g., "CR/123/2024") | Should match the charge sheet number |
| **Defendant's Name** | Your full name | Is it spelled correctly? |
| **Date of Order** | When the bail order was made | Should be on or after your first appearance |
| **Bail Amount** | How much money/property is required (e.g., "₦500,000 with two sureties") | Is this what the judge announced in court? |
| **Number of Sureties** | How many people must stand as guarantors for you (e.g., "two sureties in the sum of ₦250,000 each") | Does this match what was ordered? |
| **Conditions of Bail** | Rules you must follow while on bail (e.g., "surrender passport", "report to police station weekly") | Read carefully — breaking these can send you back to custody |
| **Sureties' Requirements** | What your sureties must provide (e.g., valid ID, proof of address, oath) | Make sure your sureties understand what's required |
| **Signature** | Signature of the judge or magistrate who made the order | Must be signed and dated |
| **Court Stamp** | Official stamp of the court | Should have the court's seal/stamp |

**Common bail conditions (wetin pesin fit see):**
 - Surrender your passport (if you get one)
 - You fit report to a designated police station at specified times (e.g, weekly or monthly)
 - No leave the city/state without court permission
 - Provide one or more sureties(guarantors) who fit sign the bail bond
 - No contact witnessess or interfere with the case
 - Reside for a specific address while you dey on bail4

**How to check if e dey correct:**

- [ ] Shey your name dey spelt correctly?
- [ ] Shey the case number macth your charge sheet?
- [ ] Shey the bail amount na the one wey the judge announced?
- [ ] Shey the met conditions dey reasonable and na wetin they discussed for court ? 
- [ ] Shey the name of the sureties dey corect?
- [ ] Shey e signed by the judge/magistrate?
- [ ] Shey e get court stamp/seal?
- [ ] Shey the date dey accurate?

**Wetin pesin fit do if somethig dey wrong:**

1. **Tell your lawyer immediately** - They fit apply to vary (change) teh bail order
2. **No sign the bail bond**  - no sign the bail bond until errors are corrected
3. **Ask the court whether them fit correct the order** - minor errors(typos) fit dey fixed
4. **If bail amount dey different** -  if the bail amount dey different from wetin the judge announce then, your lawyer fir raise am before the court
5. **Request a certified true copy** - ensure say you always get officail to give you copy from the court registry

**Red Flags(Suspicious changes wey you fit look out for):**

**High Priorities(Could indicate tampering):**
- Bail amount fit later change without your knowledge or court hearing
- Conditions fit dey added wey the judge no mention
- Your name or case number altered
- Missing judge's signature or court stamp
- Date no match when them grant you bail
- Sureties' requirementss changed (e.g, from 2 sureties to 4)

**Medium Priorities:**
- Spelling mistakes wey dey your namne or address
- Wrong date fornat
- Typographical errors in amounts (e.g., #50000 vs 500000)

**If you see any suspicious changes:**
1. Compare am with any notes wey you take for court when them grant you bail
2. Ask your lawyer say make him/her verify with the court regittry
3. Request for a certified true copy from court
4. No pay any amount or bring any sureties until the order dey verified

**Your rights regarding bail:**
- Right to be considered for bail (ACJA 2015, Section 158)
- Right to know the bail conditions clearly
- Right to apply for variation of bail if conditions are too harsh
- Right to have bail reconsidered if your circumstances change
- Right to be released once bail conditions are met (ACJA 2015, Section 171)


**Important notes about bail:**

**Bail is NOT automatic for all offences:**
- **Capital offences** (e.g., murder, armed robbery): Bail is at the judge's discretion (ACJA S.161)
- **Offences punishable by 3+ years**: Bail may be granted based on circumstances (ACJA S.162)
- **Offences punishable by less than 3 years**: You're generally entitled to bail (ACJA S.163)

**What fit happen if you break bail conditions:**
- Your bail fit dey revoked
- Them fit arrest you return you back to custody
- Your sureties fit lose their money/property
- You fit face additional charges

**Wetin dey happen to bail money:**
- If you attend all court dates: Bail is discharged, sureties are released
- If you skip court: Bail money/property may be forfeited to the court

**Sample Bail Order format:**
IN THE HIGH COURT OF LAGOS STATE
HOLDEN AT IKEJA

CR/123/2024

THE PEOPLE OF LAGOS STATE vs. JOHN DOE

ORDER FOR BAIL

Upon the application of the defendant for bail, and upon the defendant entering into a recognizance in the sum of ₦500,000 with two sureties in the sum of ₦250,000 each, it is ordered as follows:

1. The defendant is hereby granted bail in the sum of ₦500,000 with two (2) sureties.

2. Each surety must be a person of good character and provide:

   - Valid means of identification

   - Proof of address

   - Sworn affidavit of means

3. Conditions of bail:

   - The defendant shall surrender his passport to the court

   - The defendant shall report weekly to the nearest police station

   - The defendant shall not leave Lagos State without permission of this court

   - The defendant shall attend all court hearings

4. This bail is granted pursuant to Section 162 of the Administration of Criminal Justice Act, 2015.

Dated this 25th day of March 2024

Hon. [ ]

High Court of Lagos State

[Court Stamp]
