## Important Disclaimers

**This app is not a lawyer.**
It provides general information about your rights, not legal advice for your specific case.

**When you must see a real lawyer:**
- If you're being charged with a serious crime
- If you're being held in custody and don't know what to do
- If your rights are being violated
- If you're being asked to sign documents you don't understand

**How to find legal help:**
- Network of University Legal Aid Institutions (NULAI) Nigeria — runs 14+ university-based law clinics offering free pro-bono legal services to underserved communities. https://nulai.org
- WACOL (Women's Aid Collective) — Nigeria's largest legal aid provider specifically for women and girls; offices/mobile clinics in Katsina, Ebonyi, Anambra, Bayelsa, Benue, Enugu, Rivers. https://wacolnigeria.org
- National Human Rights Commission (NHRC) — federal body for reporting human rights violations (including police mistreatment/custody abuse); toll-free short code 6472, or 08006472428, Mon–Fri 8am–4pm; info@nhrc.gov.ng; https://nigeriarights.gov.ng

**Nigerian Bar Association (NBA):**
- Website: nigerianbar.org.ng
- No verified current phone number found — direct users to the website's contact page rather than a number I can't confirm is active.

**Free legal clinics:**
- NBA Annual General Conference Legal Aid Clinic — runs annually, free walk-in legal advice, staffed by volunteers speaking English, Yoruba, Hausa, Igbo, and Pidgin
- Police Duty Solicitors Scheme (PDSS) — places duty solicitors for people in police custody — directly relevant to your app's use case

**Emergency situations:**
If you're in immediate danger or being treated unfairly, tell someone you trust and ask to speak to a lawyer as soon as possible.
