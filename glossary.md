# Legal Terminology Glossary (English, Yoruba, Hausa)

## Core Terms

| English | Yoruba | Hausa | Definition |
|---------|--------|-------|------------|
| **Arrest** | Ìmú ọlọ́pàá / Gbigba ẹni lọ | Kama | When police take you into custody because they suspect you committed a crime |
| **Charge Sheet** | Ìwé ẹ̀sùn | Takardar Tuhuma | Formal document listing the crime(s) you're accused of |
| **Bail** | Ìdádúró / Bail | Bail / Saki | Release from custody while waiting for trial, usually with conditions |
| **Court** | Ilé-ẹjọ́ | Kotu | Place where legal cases are heard and decided |
| **Judge** | Adájọ́ | Alkali | Person who presides over court and makes decisions |
| **Lawyer** | Agbẹjọ́rò | Lauya | Legal professional who represents you in court |
| **Interpreter** | Oníṣe ìtumọ̀ | Mai Fassara | Person who translates between languages in court |
| **Remand** | Ìpamọ́ / Ìfi sí àtìmọ́ | Tsare | When court orders you to stay in custody until next hearing |
| **Surety** | Ẹlẹ́rìí / Onídùrògarantii | Mai tabbaci | Person who guarantees you'll return to court if you're on bail |
| **Sentence** | Ìdájọ́ / Ẹ̀wọ̀n | Hukunci | Punishment given by the court if you're found guilty |
| **Acquittal** | Ìdáre jẹ́ | Tsabta | When court finds you not guilty and you're freed |
| **Conviction** | Ẹ̀bi / Ìdájọ́ ẹ̀bi | Laifi | When court finds you guilty of a crime |
| **Appeal** | Ẹ̀bẹ̀ / Ìpè sí ilé-ẹjọ́ gíga | Ƙara | Asking a higher court to review and change the decision |
| **Warrant** | Ìwé àṣẹ / Ìwé ìmú | Takardar Kama | Court order allowing police to arrest you or search property |
| **Summons** | Ìpè / Ìwé ìpè | Kiran Kotu | Official notice ordering you to appear in court |
| **Evidence** | Ẹ̀rí | Shaida | Information or objects used to prove guilt or innocence |
| **Witness** | Ẹlẹ́rìí | Shaida | Person who saw something relevant to the case |
| **Plea** | Ẹ̀bẹ̀ / Ìpè | Ƙarar Gwaskiya | Your response to the charge (guilty or not guilty) |
| **Prosecution** | Ìgbésẹ̀ ìjọba / Ìfi ẹ̀sun kan | Tuhamar Gwamnati | The side trying to prove you committed the crime |
| **Defendant** | Ẹni tí a fi ẹ̀sun kan | Wanda aka tuhuma | You — the person accused of the crime |
|**Answer** | ehehehehehehehhe | dhhdhdhdhd | The formal written statement by a defendant in a civil case that responds to a complaint, articulating the grounds for defense.

## Common Phrases

| English | Yoruba | Hausa |
|---------|--------|-------|
| "I plead not guilty" | "Mo kò jẹ̀bi" | "Ba ni da laifi" |
| "I want a lawyer" | "Mo fẹ́ agbẹjọ́rò" | "Ina bukatar lauya" |
| "I don't understand" | "Mi ò loye" | "Ba ni fahimce ba" |
| "I need an interpreter" | "Mo nílò oníṣe ìtumọ̀" | "Ina bukatar mai fassara" |
| "When is my next court date?" | "Nígbà wo ni ẹjọ́ mi tókàn?" | "Yaushe zan sake bayyana a kotu?" |
| "Can I get bail?" | "Ṣé mo lè ní bail?" | "Zan iya samun bail?" |
