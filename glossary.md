# Legal Terminology Glossary (English, Yoruba, Hausa)

## Core Terms

| English | Yoruba | Hausa | Igbo | Definition |
|---------|--------|-------|------------| ------------|
| 1.  **Arrest** | Ìmú ọlọ́pàá / Gbigba ẹni lọ | Kama |Njide |  When police take you into custody because they suspect you committed a crime |
| 2. **Charge Sheet** | Ìwé ẹ̀sùn | Takardar Tuhuma | Akwụkwọ ebubo |Formal document listing the crime(s) you're accused of |
|3. **Bail** | Ìdádúró / Bail | Bail / Saki | NgọziỤlọikpe |Release from custody while waiting for trial, usually with conditions |
| 4. **Court** | Ilé-ẹjọ́ | Kotu | Onyeikpe | Place where legal cases are heard and decided |
| 5. **Judge** | Adájọ́ | Alkali | Ọkaiwu |Person who presides over court and makes decisions |
| 6. **Lawyer** | Agbẹjọ́rò | Lauya | Onye ntụgharị okwu|"Legal professional who represents you in court |
|7.**Interpreter** | Oníṣe ìtumọ̀ | Mai Fassara | onye ntụgharị okwu| Person who translates between languages in court |
| 8. **Remand** | Ìpamọ́ / Ìfi sí àtìmọ́ | Tsare |nchegharị | When court orders you to stay in custody until next hearing |
| 9. **Surety** | Ẹlẹ́rìí / Onídùrògarantii | Mai tabbaci | nkwa|Person who guarantees you'll return to court if you're on bail |
| 10. **Sentence** | Ìdájọ́ / Ẹ̀wọ̀n | Hukunci | ahịrịokwu | Punishment given by the court if you're found guilty |
| 11. **Acquittal** | Ìdáre jẹ́ | Tsabta | nhapu|When court finds you not guilty and you're freed |
| **Conviction** | Ẹ̀bi / Ìdájọ́ ẹ̀bi | Laifi | nkwenye |When court finds you guilty of a crime |
| 13. **Warrant** | Ìwé àṣẹ / Ìwé ìmú | Takardar Kama | akwụkwọ ikike |Court order allowing police to arrest you or search property |
|14.**Summons** | Ìpè / Ìwé ìpè | Kiran Kotu | akwukwo ikpoikpe|Official notice ordering you to appear in court |
| 15. **Evidence** | Ẹ̀rí | Shaida | |Information or objects used to prove guilt or innocence |
| 16. **Witness** | Ẹlẹ́rìí | Shaida | Onyeàmà| Person who saw something relevant to the case |
| 17. **Plea** | Ẹ̀bẹ̀ / Ìpè | Ƙarar Gwaskiya | Biko |Your response to the charge (guilty or not guilty) |
|18.**Prosecution** | Ìgbésẹ̀ ìjọba / Ìfi ẹ̀sun kan | Tuhamar Gwamnati | Ikpe |The side trying to prove you committed the crime |
| 19. **Defendant** | Ẹni tí a fi ẹ̀sun kan | Wanda aka tuhuma | Onye a na-ebo ebubo |You — the person accused of the crime |
|20. **Answer** | Ìdáhùn | Amsa |Azịza |The formal written statement by a defendant in a civil case that responds to a complaint, articulating the grounds for defense.
